# Team Agreement

## Stop Doing

- Reverting teammates' changes without discussion or justification.
- Misrepresenting individual progress and failing to commit completed work.
- Leaving work until the last minute, leaving no buffer for unexpected issues such as internet outages.
- Mishandling user data in ways that risk exposing private information (e.g., email addresses, SSNs, banking details).

## Each Member's 15% Commitment

- **Mike:** I will back up our documents locally every week.
- **Joe:** I will configure the GitHub repository to require reviews for changes to the main branch.
- **Dre:** I will proactively engage myself and my teammates to make steady progress on our individual sprint tasks each week, keeping us well ahead of our deadlines.

## Rules for Blocker Explanation

- Any merge, revert, or update to the main branch requires approval from at least one other team member.
- All members must demonstrate progress at least once every two weeks.
- All members must report their progress honestly.
